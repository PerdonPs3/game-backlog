How these picks work

Ratings of 4 stars or more favor shared genres; ratings of 2 stars or less reduce them. Wishlist games add a lighter signal. Genre and platform filters apply to every result. Personalized picks require a specific gameplay match or a shared theme and perspective. Adventure and Indie alone never qualify. Known perspective, horror and play-mode conflicts are excluded. These are catalog matches, not predictions of enjoyment.

Your ratings and wishlist stay on Vestigium. Only public game IDs, genres and platform filters go to the existing catalog provider.
